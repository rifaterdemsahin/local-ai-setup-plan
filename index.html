The Free Electricity Advantage

Running 7 GPUs 24/7 usually costs ~£200+/mo in the UK. With free power at your storage unit, your only ongoing hurdle is the £450 rent. This massive advantage allows you to run token-heavy reasoning tasks (DeepSeek-R1) all night for free.

The "Zero-Token" Shortcut

To instantly beat the £450/mo cloud break-even without needing 1.3 Billion tokens of your own usage, host private UK email bots for 2 local businesses at £225/mo each. This covers 100% of your rent, making your own tokens completely free.
